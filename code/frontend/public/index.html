What CampusSwap does

Students list the hostel rooms they would rather live in. CampusSwap looks for chains of students who can all move at once, so a swap does not need two people who want each other's rooms.

Everyone in a chain confirms, then hostel staff approve the move. Faculty swap weekly lecture slots the same way.
