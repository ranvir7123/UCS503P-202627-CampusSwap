Automatic rounds

Run a saved room matching round on a timer, so students do not wait for someone to press Run.

How rounds work

  • scheduleEveryone in a chain has 48 hours to confirm. If anyone declines or does not answer, the chain is cancelled and the others go back to the pool.
  • apartmentStudents can only rank rooms in hostels they are allowed to live in. This is checked when they send their request, so a round never suggests a move that breaks hostel rules.
  • verifiedWhen everyone has confirmed, the swap waits for your approval. Approving changes the rooms in CampusSwap's records, and the approval form (PDF) becomes ready to print.
