    Demo accounts

    Every account below exists after run.py --reset. The password for all of them is campus123. All names, roll numbers and emails are made up.

    • Sign in as a student with the Student tab, as the warden with the Hostel staff tab, and as a teacher with the Faculty tab.
    • Students can only see and rank rooms in hostels of their own gender (J and M: men; K and N: women).
    • "In demo pool" means the person already has an open request when the app starts.
    • Tracking IDs carry the year the demo data was loaded (2026 below).
